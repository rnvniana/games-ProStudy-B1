REPORTED SPEECH 💬

Practice direct and reported speech in style

Task 1: Write what the speakers said

💡 Example: Our teacher reminded us not to leave our bags on the bus.
➜ He said, "Don't leave your bags on the bus."
1. My brother persuaded me to try a new hairstyle. should
2. The inspector asked us to show him our tickets. please
3. My cousin convinced me not to put my address on the website. shouldn't
4. I told my friends not to text me in the morning. mustn't
5. The police officer ordered the driver to get out of his car. please
6. I asked my sister to help me with the washing-up. please
7. My friend encouraged me to join the sports club. should
8. My mum advised me to phone the school. should
9. The fire officer warned us not to go into the house. mustn't

Task 2: Report what each speaker said

💡 Example: Use reporting verbs like order, warn, advise, remind, ask, encourage, persuade, tell.
1. The detective shouted to the thief, "Put the gun on the floor!" order
2. The electrician said to us, "You mustn't open the box." warn
3. The hairdresser said to me, "You shouldn't use this shampoo." advise
4. I texted my friends, "Remember to come to my party." remind
5. The teacher said to the students, "Please tell me your names." ask
6. I whispered to my brother, "Please don't tell anyone!" ask
7. My friend said to me, "You should tell your mum about your problem." encourage
8. My dad said to my brother, "You really should apply for another job." persuade
9. My mum said to my dad, "Don't invite the neighbours on Saturday." tell
